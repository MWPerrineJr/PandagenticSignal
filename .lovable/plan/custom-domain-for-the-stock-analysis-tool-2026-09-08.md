## Goal
Buy and connect `pandagenticsiignal.com` as the custom web address for this project.

## Steps
1. Purchase `pandagenticsiignal.com` through Lovable's domain registrar.
2. After purchase, Lovable will automatically connect the domain to this project.
3. Set the purchased domain as the primary domain so it becomes the public URL.
4. Verify the domain status becomes Active and share the final URL.
